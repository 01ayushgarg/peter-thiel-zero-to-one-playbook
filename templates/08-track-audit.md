# Template 08 · Track audit

Check whether your plan is a plan or a track. Source: `references/13-tracks-credentials-and-the-fellowship.md`,
`references/09-mimesis-and-thinking-for-yourself.md` [HAM16, FQA14, CWT15]. The scoring is our reading.

His test, from 2016: did the plan look "less like a plan for the future and more like an alibi for the present"?
