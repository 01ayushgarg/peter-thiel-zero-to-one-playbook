# Template 06 · The ten-year test (last mover analysis)

Will you still be the leading company in 10 to 20 years? Most of your value depends on it. Source:
`references/04-last-mover-and-durability.md`, `references/08-definite-optimism-and-planning.md` [YCL5,
CS183-03, CS183-04, CS183-13].

The question: "why will this still be the leading company in ten, fifteen, twenty years from now" [YCL5]

## 1 · Where does your value come from? (rough DCF)

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Profit (or expected profit) next year | $ |
| Growth rate, next 5 years | % |
| Growth rate, years 6 to 10 | % |
| Growth rate after year 10 | % |
| Discount rate | % |
| **Share of total value from year 10 onward** | **%** |

His PayPal numbers, March 2001: about 27 months old, growing 100% a year, discounted at 30%; "about three
quarters of the value of the business as of 2001 came from cash flows in years 2011 and beyond." [YCL5]

If your answer is above 50%, durability matters more than this year's growth.
