# Template 04 · Distribution math

Check that a customer is worth more than it costs to get one, place yourself on the sales spectrum, and pick
one channel. Source: `references/05-distribution.md` [CS183-09, CS183-15].

"Poor distribution—not product—is the number one cause of failure." [CS183-09, from Blake Masters' class notes]

## 1 · Customer lifetime value (CLV)

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| Average revenue per customer per month | $ |
| Gross margin | % |
| Average customer lifetime (months) | |
| **CLV** = revenue × margin × lifetime | **$** |

The notes' example [CS183-09]: $40 a month × 24 months = $960 revenue; at 40% margin, CLV is $384.

fix before scaling.
